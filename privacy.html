Gary Local Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Gary Local is a privately operated personal AI assistant intended for authorized personal use.

Google Account Access

With authorization, Gary Local accesses Google Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, and Sheets to perform tasks requested by its owner.

Data Use

Google account information is used to perform authorized tasks such as preparing briefings, managing schedules, and working with documents.

Data Storage and Sharing

Data may be processed or stored on the owner's private systems and by configured service providers required to perform authorized tasks. Data is not sold or used for advertising.

Access and Revocation

The owner can revoke Google access through Google Account security settings and request deletion of locally retained data.
